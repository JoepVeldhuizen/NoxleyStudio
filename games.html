Forge Frenzy

In Forge Frenzy, your goal is to prepare as many orders as possible as quickly as you can. The orders consist of different weapons, such as swords, hammers, and battle axes. There is a timer counting down, so you need to work fast and complete as many orders as possible before time runs out. The faster you work and the more orders you finish, the better your score! The main goal is to make and deliver as many weapons as possible within the time limit.
