About Noxley Studio

An independent game studio from the Netherlands, making games we would want to play ourselves.

Who we are

Noxley Studio is a small independent game developer based in the Netherlands. We design, build and release our own games, from the first idea to the final boss fight. Our first game is Forge Frenzy, a blacksmith game where you smith, upgrade and sell your way through new worlds.

Noxley, the founder and the inventor of the game, is proudly and completely gay, and you can tell: the studio is loud, colourful and welcoming to everyone. Forge Frenzy started as a doodle of a blacksmith with a very fabulous apron, and it grew from there.

A few things about Noxley: he thinks every hero deserves a rainbow skin, he has strong opinions about which boss fight has the best outfit, and he claims the Ice Troll only lost because it had no sense of style. Pride is never a side quest here.

We are a small team, so every game gets our full attention. We listen to players and keep improving what we release.
